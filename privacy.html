White Zero: Survivor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version: White Zero: Survivor has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress is saved only on your device, in an encrypted file. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. The only third-party services in the app are Google AdMob (for optional rewarded ads) and Google Play (for optional purchases), which operate under Google's own policies.

Who we are

White Zero: Survivor is developed by Didier Canovas ("we", "us"). Contact: whitezero.dev.ops@protonmail.com.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal information. Specifically:

  • Game save. Your progress (unlocks, currency, settings) is stored in an encrypted file on your device only. It is never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it (unless your device's own backup service preserves it — see below).
  • Player code. The game generates a random support code (e.g. WZ-XXXX-XXXX) stored inside your local save. It contains no personal information. If you email us, the code helps us assist you; sharing it is optional.
  • No analytics. The app contains no analytics or tracking SDK from us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. When an ad is served, Google may process device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, and coarse device data to deliver and measure ads, under Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices. Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can decline. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Purchasing the "No Ads" option removes ad prompts entirely.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Purchase entitlements are recorded in your local save. For refunds, Google Play's refund policies apply.

Network requests

On launch, the game may download a small news/mailbox file from our public content site (hosted by GitHub Pages) to deliver in-game messages and gifts. This is a standard web request: the host may see your IP address in ordinary server logs, as with any website. No account, identifier, or gameplay data is sent with the request.

Device backups

Your device's operating system may include the game's save file in its own backup service (such as Android Backup tied to your Google account). That backup is controlled by your device settings and Google's policies, not by us.

Permissions

  • Internet — used only for ads, purchases, and the news/mailbox file above.
  • Vibration — used for optional haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ad serving follows Google's policies for the game's store age rating.

Your choices and data deletion

  • All game data lives on your device — uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • Ads consent (where shown) can be changed or withdrawn in-game.
  • If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time via the contact address above.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change (for example if cloud saves or new services are added), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised before the change ships.

White Zero: Survivor is based on the novel White Zero: The Promised Beach by Didier Canovas.
